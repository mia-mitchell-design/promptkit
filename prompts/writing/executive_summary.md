## Executive Summary Generator

**What it solves**

Generates structured, polished summaries for reports, documentation, or presentations.

**When to use it**

Any time you need a concise, high-level view for stakeholders.

---

### 📌 Full Prompt

```diff
Write an executive summary based on the content below.

Requirements:
- Future tense (if describing upcoming work)
- Professional and neutral tone
- 4–6 concise paragraphs
- Focus on: problem, context, approach, results, next steps

Format:
- Overview
- Problem Summary
- Methodology
- Findings
- Next Steps
```

---

### **Why it works**

It gives you structured, readable, and manager-friendly overviews of your work.
