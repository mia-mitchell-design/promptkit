## First-Day Onboarding Guide

**What it solves**

Helps you understand *where to start* when joining a team or inheriting a project.  You give it any repo or description, and it gives you an onboarding plan.

---

### 📌 Full Prompt

```vbnet
Act as a senior engineer helping me onboard into a new codebase.

Based on the project/file structure I provide, create:
1. A 1–2 paragraph mental model of the system
2. A prioritized reading order for the repo, including entry point
3. The minimum set of files I must understand first
4. The core data structures or models to learn
5. “Danger zones” (fragile or legacy components)
6. Realistic first tasks to ramp up quickly
7. Questions to ask teammates to fill gaps

Format:
- Mental Model
- Reading Order & Entry Point
- Key Files
- Core Models & Data Structures
- Risks / Danger Zones
- Suggested Starter Tasks
- Questions for the Team

```

---

### Why it works

It mimics mentorship — what a staff engineer would tell a new team member.
